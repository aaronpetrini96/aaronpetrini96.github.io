IRFx

A fast, practical tool for modern guitarists.

A guitar and bass cabinet impulse response (IR) loader with several extra processing features.

Main view

Watch the demo Source code

Main Features

IR Loader

  • Loads up to two guitar or bass cabinet IRs. Each IR can be unloaded, muted and level-adjusted.
  • A panning control appears in stereo mode.
  • 12 dB/octave high-pass and low-pass filters with adjustable cutoff frequencies.

Tone Stack

  • A classic three-knob EQ (Bass, Mid, Treble).
  • Frequency and Q are preset, except the Mid frequency, which you can adjust.

Saturation

  • Three selectable distortion modes, with Drive and Mix knobs for intensity and dry/saturated blend.

Delay

  • Placed at the end of the chain, with Digital and Tape modes.
  • Time, Feedback and Mix controls, plus a Sync button that locks the delay to the DAW's BPM.

Extras

  • Preset save/load and global bypass.
  • Input/output gain sliders and clipping indicators.
  • Independent bypass for each section.
