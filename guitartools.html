GuitarTools

GuitarTools makes mixing guitars fast and simple.

C++ · JUCE · VST3 & AU · Oct 2026

A personal C++/JUCE project covering both DSP and interface. It bundles the processing you usually need when mixing electric guitars into one plugin.

Watch the demo Download (VST3 & AU) Source code

Features

Tone Range

  • Low Cut and High Cut filters with adjustable cutoff and selectable slopes of 12, 24, 36 and 48 dB/oct.

Clean-Up

  • Kill Reso: narrow cut for harsh resonances.
  • Clear Mud: wider cut for low-mid buildup.
  • Tame Lows: band compressor for chugs and palm mutes.
  • Each tool has a MAX switch for more aggressive processing.

Color

  • Depth: low shelf with four frequency modes and gain.
  • Presence: high shelf with four frequency modes and gain.

Extras

  • Levels with peak meters and clip indicators.
  • Match Loudness and Low Tuning.
  • Preset manager with factory and user presets.
  • Undo/Redo.
  • Resizable interface with a saveable default size.
  • Optional tooltips and Global Bypass.
